CHINTHE Core Principle

What we stand for

Four values.

01

Discipline

Every decision on the mat follows a system: not instinct, not habit, but a trained response to a known position.

02

Respect

For the art, for training partners and for our roots. The guardian stands watch because it knows what it protects.

03

Perseverance

Improve with every session. Feedback from the mat drives the system, not opinion.

04

Legacy

Rooted in Myanmar, competing worldwide. The guardian lion travels far from home and never loses its purpose.

Our story

Rooted in Myanmar. Forged through jiu-jitsu.

The Chinthe is the guardian lion that stands in pairs at the gates of Myanmar's pagodas. It doesn't attack. It protects what matters and holds its ground.

That's how we see grappling: calm, positional, and always one step ahead. CHINTHE turns that idea into a system any grappler can train with, wherever they're from.
